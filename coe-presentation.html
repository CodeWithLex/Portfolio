03 // SYSTEM TOUR

4 Core Modules in Plain Language (Zero Tech Jargon)

A streamlined digital toolkit tailored to student government operations and council workflows.

01
Executive Control Tower (Dashboard)

A clean executive dashboard showing 4 live metric tiles: Total Income Received, Total Expenses Incurred, Remaining Usable Balance, and Earmarked Special Funds.

→ Instant birds-eye status for the Governor & Treasurer at a glance
02
Events & Project Budget Ledger

Allocate budget ceilings per project (e.g., Engineering Week, General Assembly, Sports Fest, Seminars). Live progress bars warn officers before exceeding budget caps.

→ Prevents project coordinators from accidental overspending
03
Digital Receipt Vault ("Shoebox Killer")

Snap photo receipts directly from smartphones during purchases. Attach vendor, date, and description. High-res proofs remain permanently searchable.

→ No more faded thermal paper or lost receipt envelopes
04
1-Click Institutional Liquidation Export

Generates ready-to-print financial statements, balance ledgers, and itemized expenditure sheets with official signatory blocks for the Governor, Treasurer, and Auditor.

→ Cuts council liquidation prep time by over 85%
04 // FIELD SCENARIO

Real-World Case: Annual Engineering Week Liquidation

Comparing a major college event managed manually versus with the COE LGU Portal.

BEFORE: MANUAL PROCESS
  • ₱120,000 budget distributed across 8 committee chairs for stages, food, and prizes.
  • 3 receipts got soaked in water during field setup; 2 thermal receipts faded into white paper.
  • Council Treasurer spent 14 nights sorting crumpled slips and calculating a ₱3,850 gap.
  • Final liquidation submission took 24 days after the event concluded.
Result: Stressed officers, audit delays, and budget hold-ups.
VS
AFTER: COE LGU PORTAL
  • Event registered with ₱120,000 budget ceiling locked in the system.
  • Committee leads took smartphone photos of receipts right at the store checkout counter.
  • Executive dashboard showed real-time remaining balance as each purchase was submitted.
  • Treasurer clicked "Export Liquidation Report": generated with all 26 verified receipt scans in 3 minutes!
Result: 100% audit accuracy, zero discrepancies, peaceful handovers.
