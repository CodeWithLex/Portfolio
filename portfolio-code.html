Engineering Principles

Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

  1. 01

    Subtract until it breaks

    Remove everything unessential to the user's intent — then stop one step before it breaks.

  2. 02

    Motion adds meaning

    Animation explains relationships and state transitions. It never just decorates.

  3. 03

    Content is interface

    The best interface gets out of the way — the data, controls, and imagery do the talking.
