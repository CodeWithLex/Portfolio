COE LGU Transparency & Student Portal

The Problem & Stakeholders:

Student council finances (₱) and fee allocations at the College of Engineering were managed through chaotic spreadsheets and manual receipts, causing student distrust and slow administrative approvals.

What I Built & Architectural Role:

A full-stack governance portal with institutional @g.cjc.edu.ph authentication, real-time Philippine Peso (₱) financial ledger with over-budget alerts, CSV bulk student roster import, and an immutable admin audit log.

Hard Technical Problem Solved:

Engineered client-side computation for the Philippine 1.0–5.0 grading scale and instant financial balance reconciliations without expensive server recalculation overhead.

Real Outcome & Impact:

Live deployment on a custom .engineer domain serving the College of Engineering council and student body.

Process & Engineering Craft

Disciplined engineering, zero fluff.

  1. 01

    Spec & schema before code

    Model data structures, state transitions, and edge-case contracts on paper before opening the IDE.

  2. 02

    Phase-gated validation

    Build in discrete, testable milestones — database schemas first, API/logic second, UI ergonomics third.

  3. 03

    Zero-bloat execution

    Favor clean web APIs, custom CSS properties, and direct database queries over heavy runtime bundle dependencies.

  4. 04

    Community utility first

    Build what solves an actual institutional bottleneck — for student orgs, lab managers, and local communities.

Creative Philosophy

Observe first, shoot second.

  1. 01

    Authenticity over polish

    The best frames aren't staged — they're discovered. Waiting patiently for the honest, unforced moment.

  2. 02

    Light tells the story

    Shooting with prime lenses at wide apertures so ambient light and genuine mood carry the emotion.

  3. 03

    Edit with restraint

    Post-processing removes distractions, never alters truth. What was felt stays felt.
