CASE STUDY

Digital governance for the College of Engineering.

THE PROBLEM

Student council finances and event allocations relied on disconnected spreadsheets and paper slips. Students had zero real-time visibility into fund usage, and officers spent hours manually balancing ledgers before audits.

THE SOLUTION

A centralized, domain-gated web portal (@g.cjc.edu.ph) giving every engineering student real-time financial transparency, automated budget tracking, and instant academic progress computation.

01

₱ Real-Time Treasury

Automated income, expense, and donation tracking with built-in over-budget warnings.

02

Immutable Audit Log

Tamper-evident activity logs recording every budget transfer, disbursement, and council action.

03

Academic Progress Engine

Client-side 1.0 - 5.0 Philippine GPA calculator with instant student progress PDF export.

04

Batch Admin Operations

High-speed CSV roster parsing for bulk student verification and institutional enrollment.
